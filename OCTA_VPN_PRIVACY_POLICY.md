# OCTA VPN Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 28, 2026

## Introduction

OctaSpace ("we," "our," or "us") operates OCTA VPN, a virtual private network service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our VPN service.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. OCTA VPN is designed with a strict no-logs policy, meaning we do not monitor, record, or store your online activities.

## Information We Collect

### What We DO Collect

- **Bandwidth Usage:** We collect the amount of data (traffic) consumed by your account solely for billing purposes. This includes only the total bytes transferred and does not include any information about the content, destination, or nature of your traffic.

### What We DO NOT Collect

OCTA VPN does not collect, log, or store:

- Your browsing history or the websites you visit
- Your original IP address
- DNS queries
- Traffic destination or content
- Any data transmitted through the VPN tunnel

## How We Use Your Information

The bandwidth usage data we collect is used exclusively for:

- Calculating billing charges based on your data consumption
- Maintaining accurate account records for payment processing

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your bandwidth usage data with any third parties except:

- When required by law through valid legal process
- To protect our rights or property
- To prevent fraud or abuse of our services

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data we collect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Data Retention

Bandwidth usage data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill billing obligations and maintain accurate account records. Upon account termination, billing data is retained only as required by applicable law.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access information about your bandwidth usage
- Request deletion of your account and associated billing data
- Contact us with questions about our privacy practices

## Third-Party Services

Our VPN service connects you to the internet through our network. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services you access while using OCTA VPN.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website with an updated revision date.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

- Website: https://octa.space
- Email: hello@octa.space

**Summary:** OCTA VPN respects your privacy. We only collect bandwidth usage data for billing. We do not log your browsing activity, IP addresses, or any other personal data related to your VPN usage.
